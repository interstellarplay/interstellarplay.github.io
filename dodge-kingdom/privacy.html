Dodge Kingdom privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026. Dodge Kingdom is published by Interstellar Play, the studio name of Adrian Petre, an individual developer. Contact: play.interstellargames@gmail.com.

What the game stores

  • Your progress: levels, coins, items, pets and settings. They are saved only on your phone.
  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example, when an egg hatches or a daily gift is ready). They are local, nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android's settings.
  • Test data: the "Copy test data" button in the settings copies your recent level results and your phone model to the clipboard. Nothing is sent: you decide whether to paste it into a message.

What we don't do

The game has no account, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. It does not send your data anywhere, does not sell data and does not collect your location.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes all of it.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If a future version adds ads or purchases, this page will be updated before that version is released.
