Dodge Kingdom privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026. Dodge Kingdom is published by Interstellar Play, the studio name of Adrian Petre, an individual developer. Contact: play.interstellargames@gmail.com.

What the game stores

  • Your progress: levels, coins, items, pets and settings. They are saved only on your phone.
  • Reminders: if you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example, when an egg hatches or a daily gift is ready). They are local, nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android's settings.
  • Online profile (friends, clans, co-op and leaderboards): when you open Friends or Ranks, the game signs you in anonymously (no email, no password) and saves a public profile: your player ID (like DK-ABCD-2345), the nickname you chose, your profile picture (one of the game's characters), your best results (level, stars, kings defeated, best runs), the number of crowns and the items you wear. Other players can see it on the leaderboard and, once you are friends, on your friend page. Friend requests you send or receive are stored with it, and so are: when you last had the game open and whether you are playing (so friends see who is online), the daily gifts sent between friends, the clan you join with its trophies and clan wars, the co-op levels you cleared together with a friend, your damage to the weekly boss, and any report you send about a player's or clan's name (or about cheating), so we can check it. During online co-op, your hero's position and moves are sent live to your friend's phone through the same servers and are not stored. This is kept on Supabase servers in the European Union (Frankfurt), sent over an encrypted connection.
  • Test data: the "Copy test data" button in the settings copies your recent level results and your phone model to the clipboard. Nothing is sent: you decide whether to paste it into a message.

What we don't do

The game has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. Apart from the online profile above, it does not send your data anywhere. It does not sell data and does not collect your location, contacts or email.

Deleting your data

In the game, Profile → Friends → "Delete my online data" removes your online profile from the server, together with your friend requests, gifts, clan membership, co-op records and boss damage. To ask by email instead, write to the contact address above with your player ID (Profile → Friends). Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes everything kept on your phone.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If a future version adds ads or purchases, this page will be updated before that version is released.
