How it works

No account, no sign-up. The whole run travels inside the link.

1

Run today's track

Tap to jump, swipe down to slide. The track is new every day and the same for everyone, so every comparison is fair.

2

Send your ghost

One tap shares a link with your run inside it. Add a cheeky message: "Bet you can't beat me!"

3

They race it

Your friend opens the link and races your ghost on the same course. Then they send theirs back.
