Beat My Ghost

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026. Beat My Ghost is published by Interstellar Play, the studio name of Adrian Petre, an individual developer. Contact: play.interstellargames@gmail.com.

What we use

  • Your runs: the moments you jumped and the distance you reached. They are stored on your phone and, when you share them, inside the link you send.
  • The name you choose in the game: it appears in the links you share and, from version 1.1, on the leaderboard. It does not need to be your real name.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show ads. Personalisation depends on your consent.
  • Usage data: anonymous events through Firebase Analytics (for example, a run started or ended) to improve the game.

What we don't do

We don't ask for an account, email or password. We don't sell data. We don't collect precise location.

Consent

On first launch we ask whether ads may be personalised. If you decline, you see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice in the game's settings.

Third parties

Google AdMob, Google Firebase and Google Play Games Services, under Google's policies. From version 1.1 the leaderboard is hosted by Supabase.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data at play.interstellargames@gmail.com. Local data is removed when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over.
